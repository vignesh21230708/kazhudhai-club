📜 How to Play

🎯 Objective

Be the first to empty your hand. The last player still holding cards becomes KAZHUDHAI!

🃏 Card Ranking (Highest → Lowest)

A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2

The player holding A♠ starts the first round.

🟢 Normal Round (No Attack)

  1. Starting player plays a card — that suit becomes the starting suit.
  2. Every other player must follow that suit if they have it.
  3. If all players follow, the round ends normally.
  4. Highest-ranking card of the starting suit wins.
  5. All played cards go into the common collected section (permanently removed).
  6. The winner starts the next round.

🔴 Attack Round

  1. If a player cannot follow the starting suit, they play any other card.
  2. This triggers an ATTACK — the round ends immediately!
  3. Remaining players skip their turn.
  4. Highest card of the starting suit still wins.
  5. ALL played cards (including the attacking card) go to the winner's hand.

⚠️ Playing Your Last Card

Playing your last card does NOT immediately eliminate you. Wait for the round to finish. If an attack occurs and you win — you get cards back!
